02 / HIS JOURNEY

THE WILLIAM SALIBA STORY

Early Development

Saliba began at AS Bondy before developing through FC Montfermeil and AS Saint-Étienne. Initially a forward, he transitioned into defence and developed exceptional athleticism, tactical intelligence, and composure.

Arsenal & Loans

Arsenal signed Saliba in July 2019 and allowed him to continue his development through loan spells at Saint-Étienne, Nice, and Marseille. His 2021–22 season at Marseille earned him Ligue 1 Young Player of the Year recognition and a place in the Ligue 1 Team of the Year.

France & Recognition

After returning to Arsenal in 2022, Saliba became a first-team regular and formed an important defensive partnership with Gabriel Magalhães. He also became an important member of the France national team.
