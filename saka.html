THE BUKAYO SAKA STORY

Hale End & Early Rise

Saka joined Arsenal's Hale End Academy at the age of eight. His technical ability, balance, intelligence, and humility quickly marked him out as a special talent. He made his professional debut for Arsenal in November 2018 against Vorskla Poltava.

Arsenal Career

Saka developed into one of Arsenal's most important attacking players. His dribbling, crossing, movement, and goal contributions have made him a central figure in the club's modern resurgence. He won the FA Cup in 2020 and two FA Community Shields.

England & International Stage

Saka made his England debut in October 2020. He played in England's run to the UEFA Euro 2020 final, scored three goals at the 2022 FIFA World Cup, and continued to play an important role for England at UEFA Euro 2024.
