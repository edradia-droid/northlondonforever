02 / HIS JOURNEY

THE DIXON STORY

Arsenal Career

Dixon joined Arsenal in January 1988 and became an essential part of the club's defensive structure. He eventually made 619 appearances and became one of Arsenal's most reliable full-backs.

The Famous Back Four

Dixon formed part of Arsenal's famous defensive unit alongside Tony Adams, Steve Bould and Nigel Winterburn. Together they became synonymous with discipline, organisation and defensive excellence.

England & Media

Dixon earned 22 England caps between 1990 and 1999. After retiring in 2002, he moved into broadcasting, working with the BBC, ITV and NBC Sports as a respected football analyst.
