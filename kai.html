02 / HIS JOURNEY

THE KAI HAVERTZ STORY

Bayer Leverkusen Rise

Havertz developed through Alemannia Mariadorf and Alemannia Aachen before joining Bayer Leverkusen's academy. He made his Bundesliga debut at 17 and quickly became one of Germany's most exciting young talents.

Chelsea & Champions League Glory

Havertz joined Chelsea in 2020. He became a club history-maker by scoring the winning goal in the 2021 UEFA Champions League Final against Manchester City and later scored in the FIFA Club World Cup Final.

Arsenal Role

Havertz joined Arsenal in 2023. Initially used in midfield, he increasingly found his role as a forward, where his height, aerial ability, movement, and finishing became important elements of Arsenal's attack.
