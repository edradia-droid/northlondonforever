THE Santi STORY

Career Overview

Cazorla began his professional career with Villarreal, developing a reputation for close control and tactical intelligence. After a successful stint at Málaga, he joined Arsenal in 2012, where his vision and two-footed play made him a fan favorite.

Arsenal Legacy & Resilience

At Arsenal, Cazorla won two FA Cups and was named Arsenal’s Player of the Season in 2012–13. Severe Achilles injuries sidelined him for an extended period, but his celebrated return to top-level football became a defining part of his story.

International Career & Legacy

Cazorla earned 81 caps and scored 15 goals for Spain. He was part of the squads that won UEFA Euro 2008 and UEFA Euro 2012, and his balance, vision, set pieces, and resilience secured his place among Arsenal’s admired modern players.
