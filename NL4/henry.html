THE THIERRY STORY

Club Career & Arsenal Legacy

Henry’s professional debut came at AS Monaco under Arsène Wenger in 1994. After a brief stint with Juventus F.C., he joined Arsenal in 1999 and flourished as a forward of rare pace, intelligence, and precision. Across two spells in North London, he scored 228 goals, helping Arsenal win two Premier League titles—including the unbeaten 2003-04 “Invincibles” season—and two FA Cups.

International Success

With France, Henry was a cornerstone of the country’s “golden generation.” He won the 1998 FIFA World Cup and UEFA Euro 2000, added the 2003 FIFA Confederations Cup Golden Ball and Golden Shoe, and featured in four World Cups.

Later Career & Coaching

Henry joined FC Barcelona in 2007, winning the 2008–09 UEFA Champions League as part of Pep Guardiola’s treble-winning side. He concluded his playing days with the New York Red Bulls (2010–2014). Post-retirement, Henry turned to coaching.
