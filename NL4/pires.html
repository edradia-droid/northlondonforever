THE ROBERT STORY

Career & Playing Style

Pirès began professionally with FC Metz in 1992, winning the 1996 League Cup before joining Olympique de Marseille. In 2000 he transferred to Arsenal, where under Arsène Wenger he became central to the “French Connection” era. His combination play, movement, and precise finishing helped define the club’s fluid attacking style.

Arsenal & The Invincibles

Between 2000 and 2006, Pirès played 284 matches and scored 84 goals for Arsenal. He twice won the Premier League (2001–02, 2003–04) and three FA Cups (2002, 2003, 2005). The 2003–04 unbeaten season cemented his legacy as part of the “Invincibles.”

International Success & Legacy

For France, Pirès won the 1998 FIFA World Cup and the 2000 UEFA European Championship. He added two FIFA Confederations Cup titles and was included in Pelé’s FIFA 100 list in 2004. After Arsenal he played for Villarreal and Aston Villa before retiring in 2015.
