Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang

Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang is a Gabonese professional soccer player known for his speed, finishing, and goal-scoring consistency. Born on June 18 1989 in Laval, France, he rose to prominence with Borussia Dortmund and later starred as captain and striker for Arsenal FC. As of 2026, he plays for Olympique de Marseille in Ligue 1.

Career trajectory

Aubameyang began in AC Milan’s youth setup before loans in France led to a breakthrough with AS Saint-Étienne, where his pace and clinical finishing earned a move to Borussia Dortmund in 2013. In Germany he scored over 100 goals, won the DFB-Pokal, and became one of Europe’s fastest strikers. He joined Arsenal in 2018 for a then-club-record fee, winning the FA Cup in 2020 and sharing the Premier League Golden Boot in 2018-19. Later stints at Barcelona and Chelsea were followed by a prolific season with Saudi club Al-Qadsiah (17 league goals in 2024-25) before returning to Marseille in 2025.

Playing style and impact

Renowned for his explosive acceleration and intelligent off-ball movement, Aubameyang excels at attacking space behind defenses. In his mid-30s he evolved into a more positional, poaching striker—still effective due to elite timing and finishing. His professionalism and leadership have made him a mentor to younger forwards at Marseille.

International career

Debuting for Gabon in 2009, Aubameyang has scored over 40 international goals, captaining the side across multiple Africa Cup of Nations campaigns. He won the African Footballer of the Year award in 2015 and remains one of the continent’s most celebrated forwards.

Legacy

With more than 350 career goals across six countries, Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang ranks among Africa’s greatest modern strikers—admired for his longevity, adaptability, and consistent impact at the top levels of European football.
