Santi Cazorla

Santi Cazorla (born December 13, 1984) is a Spanish professional footballer known for his technical ability, ambidexterity, and creativity as a midfielder. Celebrated during his spell at Arsenal, he has enjoyed a long career across Europe, now playing for Real Oviedo in Spain’s top flight.

Career overview

Cazorla began his professional career with Villarreal CF in 2003, developing a reputation for close control and tactical intelligence. After a successful stint at Málaga CF, he joined Arsenal in 2012, where his vision and two-footed play made him a fan favorite. He won two FA Cup titles and was named Arsenal’s Player of the Season in 2012–13. Persistent Achilles injuries sidelined him for nearly two years, but he made a celebrated comeback with Villarreal in 2018 before later moving to Al Sadd SC and eventually Real Oviedo.

International career

Cazorla earned 81 caps and scored 15 goals for the Spain national football team between 2008 and 2019. He was part of the squads that won the UEFA Euro 2008 and UEFA Euro 2012, contributing both creativity and versatility to Spain’s golden generation.

Playing style and legacy

Renowned for balance, vision, and mastery with either foot, Cazorla has been lauded for his playmaking and set-piece precision. His resilience after severe injury and return to top-level football made him a symbol of perseverance. At Arsenal and Villarreal alike, he remains admired for both his artistry and professionalism.
