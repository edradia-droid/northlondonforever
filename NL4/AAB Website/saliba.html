William Saliba

William Saliba is a French professional soccer player who plays as a central defender for Arsenal and the France national football team. Known for his composure, pace, and ball-playing ability, he has become one of the Premier League’s most consistent defenders and a cornerstone of Arsenal’s modern resurgence under Mikel Arteta.

Early life and development

Raised in Bondy, a suburb of Paris famed for nurturing talents like Kylian Mbappé, Saliba began playing at AS Bondy before joining FC Montfermeil and later the academy of AS Saint-Étienne in 2016. Initially a forward, he transitioned to defense during his youth development, combining athleticism with tactical intelligence and calm distribution.

Professional career

Saliba made his senior debut with Saint-Étienne in 2018 and quickly drew attention for his maturity. Arsenal signed him in July 2019, loaning him back to the French club for experience. Subsequent loans to OGC Nice and Olympique de Marseille helped refine his game—his 2021-22 season at Marseille earned him the UNFP Ligue 1 Young Player of the Year and a Ligue 1 Team of the Year place. He returned to Arsenal to become a first-team regular in 2022–23, anchoring one of England’s best defenses and earning a long-term contract through 2027.

International career

Saliba debuted for France’s senior side in March 2022 and featured at the 2022 FIFA World Cup. He later became a key figure during France’s run to the semifinals of UEFA Euro 2024, earning selection in the Team of the Tournament. His partnership with elite defenders such as Dayot Upamecano underscores his status as a mainstay in the national setup.

Style and recognition

Saliba is lauded for his timing in tackles, aerial strength, and ability to initiate attacks from deep. His blend of athletic power and technical control has drawn comparisons to past Arsenal greats and made him a model of the modern centre-back. Off the pitch, he has become a prominent ambassador for brands like Crocs France, reflecting his growing global profile.
