Gabriel Martinelli

Gabriel Teodoro Martinelli Silva (born June 18, 2001) is a Brazilian professional footballer who plays as a winger and forward for Arsenal F.C. and the Brazil national football team. Known for his explosive pace, high pressing, and direct attacking style, he has become one of the most dynamic wide players in the Premier League since joining Arsenal in 2019.

Early career and move to Arsenal

Martinelli began in futsal with Corinthians before joining Ituano FC’s academy in 2014. He debuted professionally at 16 and scored 10 goals in 34 matches for Ituano. Arsenal signed him in July 2019 for about £6 million. His first season delivered 10 goals, including a celebrated solo strike against Chelsea F.C. that earned the club’s Goal of the Season award and an FA Cup winner’s medal.

Development under Mikel Arteta

After recovering from a knee injury in 2020, Martinelli flourished under manager Mikel Arteta. Operating mainly from the left wing, he became vital to Arsenal’s high-intensity system, combining relentless off-ball work with improved decision-making and finishing. In 2022-23 he scored 15 league goals, equaling Roberto Firmino’s record for the most by a Brazilian in a Premier League season.

Playing style

Martinelli’s game blends acceleration, vertical dribbling, and defensive effort. He presses aggressively from the front and attacks space behind defenses. Jurgen Klopp once described him as a “talent of the century,” praising his fearlessness and work rate. His style evokes the modern two-way winger—capable of both creating and finishing.

International career

A dual Brazilian-Italian citizen, Martinelli chose to represent Brazil. He won Olympic gold with the U-23 team at the Tokyo 2020 Summer Olympics, debuted for the senior side in 2022, and appeared at the 2022 FIFA World Cup and 2024 Copa América. His early international goals reinforced his role in Brazil’s next generation of attackers.

Honors

  • With Arsenal: FA Cup (2019-20), Community Shield (2020, 2023)
  • With Brazil: Olympic Gold (2020 Tokyo)
  • Individual: Campeonato Paulista Young Player of the Year (2019)
