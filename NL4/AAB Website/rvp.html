Robin van Persie

Robin van Persie (born August 6, 1983) is a Dutch former professional footballer and current manager, best known for his prolific spell as a striker for Arsenal and the Netherlands national team. Renowned for his technical skill and lethal left foot, he became one of Europe’s most admired forwards of the 2000s and 2010s.

Arsenal years (2004–2012)

Van Persie joined Arsenal from Feyenoord in 2004 as Dennis Bergkamp’s long-term successor. Initially raw but technically gifted, he matured into a world-class striker under Arsène Wenger. Over eight seasons he scored 132 goals in 278 games, winning the FA Cup (2005) and earning both the PFA and FWA Player of the Year awards in 2012 after a 37-goal campaign that also secured the Premier League Golden Boot.

Manchester United and later career

In 2012, Van Persie moved to Manchester United, where his goals led the club to the 2012–13 Premier League title. He topped the league scoring charts again with 26 goals, collecting his second Golden Boot. After two more seasons at United, he joined Fenerbahçe in Turkey before finishing his playing career at Feyenoord, where he retired in 2019.

International career

With 102 caps and 50 goals, Van Persie is the Netherlands’ all-time leading scorer. He featured in three FIFA World Cups (2006, 2010, 2014) and two UEFA European Championships, famously scoring a spectacular diving header against Spain at the 2014 World Cup.

Coaching and legacy

After retirement, Van Persie began coaching in Feyenoord’s youth system. He became head coach of Heerenveen in 2024 and, in 2025, returned to Feyenoord as head coach. Celebrated for his elegance, intelligence, and striking precision, Van Persie remains a symbol of Dutch attacking artistry and Arsenal excellence.
