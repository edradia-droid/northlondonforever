Aaron Ramsey

Aaron Ramsey is a Welsh former professional footballer best known for his 11-year spell as a midfielder with Arsenal F.C. and his leadership of the Wales national football team. Renowned for his work rate, technique, and decisive goals, he became one of Wales’s most influential players of the 21st century.

Club career

Ramsey began at Cardiff City F.C., becoming their youngest-ever player at 16 before joining Arsenal in 2008. He won three FA Cups with the Gunners, famously scoring the winning goals in the 2014 and 2017 finals. After leaving Arsenal in 2019, he played for Juventus F.C.—winning the 2019–20 Serie A title—then for Rangers, Nice, and Cardiff. His final club was Club Universidad Nacional in Mexico.

International achievements

Ramsey debuted for Wales in 2008 and became its youngest-ever captain at 20. He starred in the nation’s landmark run to the UEFA Euro 2016 semifinals, earning a place in UEFA’s Team of the Tournament. He later represented Wales at Euro 2020 and the 2022 FIFA World Cup—the country’s first in 64 years

Playing style and legacy

A dynamic box-to-box midfielder, Ramsey combined energy with creativity, often timing late runs into the penalty area. Despite severe injuries early in his career, his resilience and big-game contributions made him a fan favorite. Many Welsh observers regard him, alongside Gareth Bale, as one of the greatest footballers in the nation’s history

Life after football

Since retiring in 2026, Ramsey has pursued coaching, including a brief stint as interim head coach of Cardiff City. He continues philanthropic work and participated in the London Marathon to raise funds for children’s health charities
