THE TONY STORY

Arsenal Career

Adams joined Arsenal as a schoolboy in 1980 and debuted at age 17 in 1983. He became club captain in 1988—the youngest in Arsenal’s history—and retained the armband for 14 years. A one-club man, he made 669 appearances and won 10 major trophies.

International Career

Between 1987 and 2000, Adams earned 66 caps for England, scoring five goals. He represented his nation at four major tournaments—Euro 1988, Euro 1996, the 1998 FIFA World Cup, and Euro 2000.

Later Management & Advocacy

After retiring in 2002, Adams managed Wycombe Wanderers, Portsmouth, and Granada CF, and held coaching or technical roles across Europe and Asia. He also founded the Sporting Chance Clinic in 2000 to support athletes.
