THE DENNIS STORY

Early Career & Ajax Years

Bergkamp joined Ajax’s youth academy at age 11 and debuted for the senior team in 1986 under Johan Cruyff. Over seven seasons, he scored more than 120 goals and won the Eredivisie, KNVB Cup, and the 1987 European Cup Winners’ Cup.

Arsenal Legacy

Joining Arsenal in 1995 for £7.5 million, Bergkamp became the linchpin of Arsène Wenger’s attacking revolution. Over 11 seasons he scored 120 goals in 423 appearances, helping the Gunners to three Premier League titles—including the unbeaten 2003–04 “Invincibles” season—and four FA Cups.

International Career & Style

Capped 79 times for the Netherlands, Bergkamp scored 37 goals, including his iconic strike against Argentina in the 1998 FIFA World Cup quarterfinals. Nicknamed the “Non-Flying Dutchman,” he soared on the field with elegance and intelligence.
