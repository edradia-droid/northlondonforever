THE PATRICK STORY

Arsenal & Premier League Dominance

Vieira joined Arsenal in 1996, recruited by Arsène Wenger. His athleticism, tackling, and distribution anchored Arsenal’s midfield for nine years. As captain, he led the “Invincibles” team that went unbeaten through the 2003–04 Premier League season. Vieira won three league titles and four FA Cups with the club.

International Success With France

Vieira debuted for France in 1997 and helped the national team win the 1998 FIFA World Cup and UEFA Euro 2000. He also featured prominently in France’s run to the 2006 World Cup final. His blend of power and tactical intelligence made him one of the era’s most complete midfielders.

Later Career & Management

After Arsenal, Vieira played for Juventus, Inter Milan—winning four Serie A titles—and finished at Manchester City, retiring in 2011. He then moved into coaching, working with Manchester City’s youth setup and New York City FC before managing in France’s Ligue 1 and England’s Premier League.
