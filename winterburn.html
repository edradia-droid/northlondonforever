02 / HIS JOURNEY

THE WINTERBURN STORY

Arsenal Career

Winterburn joined Arsenal in 1987 and quickly established himself at left-back. He went on to make 440 appearances over 13 years and became a crucial part of the club's defensive identity.

Major Honours

With Arsenal, Winterburn won three league titles, two FA Cups, a League Cup and the 1994 European Cup Winners' Cup. His reliability and work ethic made him one of the defining defenders of his era.

England & Later Career

Winterburn earned two England caps. After leaving Arsenal, he played for West Ham United and later worked in coaching and football broadcasting, becoming a respected analyst of the game.
