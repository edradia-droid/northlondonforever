THE DAVID JACK STORY

Early Career & Bolton

Jack began his senior career at Plymouth Argyle before joining Bolton Wanderers in 1920. At Bolton he scored over 140 league goals and became the first player to score at Wembley Stadium in the 1923 FA Cup Final.

Arsenal Years

In 1928, Herbert Chapman signed Jack for a record £10,890, one of football’s landmark transfer fees. Jack quickly became integral to Arsenal’s rise, scoring 124 goals in 208 appearances and helping the club win the FA Cup and three First Division titles.

Playing Style & Later Life

Jack was known for precise shooting, positional intelligence and leadership. After retiring in 1934, he managed Southend United, Middlesbrough and Shelbourne, remaining an important figure from Arsenal’s formative professional era.
