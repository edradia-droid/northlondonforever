THE ALEX JAMES STORY

Early Career

James began at Bellshill Athletic before signing with Raith Rovers in 1922. His creativity and ball control drew attention, earning a move to Preston North End in 1925, where he developed his reputation as one of Britain’s most skillful inside-forwards.

Arsenal & Tactical Influence

In 1929 he joined Arsenal under Herbert Chapman. His artistry became central to Chapman’s revolutionary WM formation. James became the creative fulcrum of a side that dominated English football, winning three league titles and two FA Cups.

International & Legacy

James earned eight caps for Scotland and was associated with the famous Wembley Wizards era. After retiring in 1937, he served in the British Army during World War II and later worked as a sports journalist. His deep-lying creative style remains part of his enduring legacy.
