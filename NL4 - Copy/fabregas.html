THE Cesc STORY

Early Career & Arsenal Rise

Fàbregas began at CE Mataró and entered FC Barcelona’s youth system in 1997. In 2003, aged 16, he joined Arsenal under Arsène Wenger, rapidly becoming a central figure. By 21 he was captain, and his precise distribution and leadership defined Arsenal’s midfield through more than 300 appearances.

Barcelona & Chelsea Success

Returning to Barcelona in 2011, Fàbregas adapted seamlessly to Pep Guardiola’s tactical structure, winning La Liga, Copa del Rey, FIFA Club World Cup, and UEFA Super Cup. Moving to Chelsea in 2014, he won two Premier League titles and became one of the league’s most accomplished creators.

International Achievements

Integral to Spain’s golden generation, Fàbregas won UEFA Euro 2008, the FIFA World Cup 2010, and UEFA Euro 2012. He also delivered the decisive assist to Andrés Iniesta in the 2010 World Cup Final.
