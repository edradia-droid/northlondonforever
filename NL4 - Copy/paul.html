THE MERSON STORY

Rise at Arsenal

Merson joined Arsenal's youth system in 1982 and made his first-team debut in 1986. Under George Graham, his dribbling, vision and creativity became an important part of Arsenal's successful team.

Major Honours

Merson helped Arsenal win the 1988–89 and 1990–91 league titles, the 1993 FA Cup and League Cup double, and the 1994 European Cup Winners' Cup. He was also named PFA Young Player of the Year in 1989.

England & Later Career

Merson represented England 21 times and appeared at Euro 1992 and the 1998 World Cup. After leaving Arsenal, he played for Middlesbrough, Aston Villa, Portsmouth and Walsall before moving into punditry.
