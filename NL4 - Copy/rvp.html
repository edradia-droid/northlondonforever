THE Robin STORY

Arsenal Years

Van Persie joined Arsenal from Feyenoord in 2004 as Dennis Bergkamp’s long-term successor. Initially raw but technically gifted, he matured into a world-class striker under Arsène Wenger. He scored 132 goals in 278 games and won the FA Cup in 2005.

Manchester United & Later Career

In 2012, Van Persie moved to Manchester United, where his goals helped secure the 2012–13 Premier League title. He later played for Fenerbahçe before finishing his playing career at Feyenoord.

International Career & Coaching

With 102 caps and 50 goals, Van Persie became the Netherlands’ all-time leading scorer. After retirement, he moved into coaching, working in Feyenoord’s youth system before taking senior coaching roles.
