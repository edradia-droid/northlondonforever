01 / CLUB TIMELINE

THE ERAS

1886 — PRESENT
1886–1913 (Formation & Early Years)

Founded as Dial Square in Woolwich; renamed Royal Arsenal, then Woolwich Arsenal; moved to Highbury in 1913

Notable figures: Founders (workers at Royal Arsenal factory)

Achievements / notes: Joined the Football League in 1893

1919–1939 (Rise to Power)

Promoted to First Division controversially in 1919; became dominant in English football

Notable figures: Herbert Chapman

Achievements / notes: Won multiple league titles and FA Cups; introduced modern tactics and professionalism

1940s–1960s (Post-war Period)

Mixed success after WWII; gradual rebuilding

Notable figures: Bertie Mee

Achievements / notes: First “Double” (League + FA Cup) in club history

1980s–1990s (George Graham Era)

Defensive discipline; strong domestic success

Notable figures: George Graham

Achievements / notes: League titles (1989, 1991); European Cup Winners’ Cup (1994)

1996–2018 (Arsène Wenger Era)

1996–2018 (Arsène Wenger Era)

Notable figures: Arsene Wenger

Achievements / notes: “Invincibles” 2003–04 season (unbeaten league); multiple FA Cups

2006 (European Highlight)

Reached UEFA Champions League final

Notable figures: Arsène Wenger, Thierry Henry

Achievements / notes: First and only UCL final (lost to Barcelona)

2006–Present (Emirates Era)

Moved to Emirates Stadium; financial transition period

Notable figures: Arsène Wenger

Achievements / notes: Continued FA Cup success; rebuilding phase

2019–Present (Modern Era)

Youth development and squad rebuild under Arteta

Notable figures: Mikel Arteta

Achievements / notes: FA Cup 2020; renewed title challenges
