02 / HIS JOURNEY

THE Pierre-Emerick STORY

Career Trajectory

Aubameyang began in AC Milan’s youth setup before loans in France led to a breakthrough with Saint-Étienne. He moved to Borussia Dortmund in 2013, where his pace and clinical finishing made him one of Europe’s fastest and most prolific strikers.

Arsenal Years

He joined Arsenal in 2018 and quickly became one of the club’s leading forwards. He won the FA Cup in 2020 and shared the Premier League Golden Boot in 2018–19, while his speed and finishing made him a decisive attacking threat.

International Career & Legacy

Aubameyang became one of Gabon’s leading forwards and won the African Footballer of the Year award in 2015. His Arsenal years remain a memorable part of the modern era, defined by goals, acceleration, and a dramatic FA Cup triumph.
