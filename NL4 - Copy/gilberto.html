THE GILBERTO STORY

Club Career & Arsenal Legacy

Gilberto Silva joined Arsenal in 2002 after impressing in Brazil. His calm positioning, intelligence and ability to protect the defence quickly made him an important part of Arsène Wenger's midfield.

During his Arsenal career, Silva won the Premier League title as part of the unbeaten 2003–04 Invincibles team and lifted the FA Cup twice.

International Success

Silva became an important member of the Brazilian national team. He played every minute of Brazil's successful 2002 FIFA World Cup campaign and helped his country win the tournament.

He later helped Brazil win the Copa América and FIFA Confederations Cup, establishing himself as one of his country's most dependable defensive midfielders.

Style & Legacy

Known as the "Invisible Wall", Silva was admired for his understated but highly effective style. Rather than seeking attention, he protected his defenders, recovered possession and kept Arsenal's midfield balanced.

His intelligence, discipline and professionalism made him an essential part of Arsenal's golden era and a beloved figure among supporters.
