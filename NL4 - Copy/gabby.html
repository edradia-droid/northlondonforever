THE GABRIEL MAGALHÃES STORY

Early Career

Gabriel began his youth career at Avaí in Brazil, making his senior debut in 2016. He later moved to Lille in France, with loan spells at Troyes and Dinamo Zagreb helping him develop into a commanding central defender.

Arsenal Career

Arsenal signed Gabriel in September 2020. He quickly established himself in Mikel Arteta's starting lineup, becoming an important defensive leader and a major threat from set pieces.

Brazil & Defensive Partnership

Gabriel earned his senior Brazil call-up in 2023. At Arsenal, his partnership with William Saliba has become central to the club's defensive strength, combining physical aggression with composure and technical quality.
