HISTORY OF ARSENAL FOOTBALL CLUB
Period Key Events Notable Figures Achievements / Notes
1886–1913 (Formation & Early Years) Founded as Dial Square in Woolwich; renamed Royal Arsenal, then Woolwich Arsenal; moved to Highbury in 1913 Founders (workers at Royal Arsenal factory) Joined the Football League in 1893
1919–1939 (Rise to Power) Promoted to First Division controversially in 1919; became dominant in English football Herbert Chapman Won multiple league titles and FA Cups; introduced modern tactics and professionalism
1940s–1960s (Post-war Period) Mixed success after WWII; gradual rebuilding Bertie Mee First “Double” (League + FA Cup) in club history
1980s–1990s (George Graham Era) Defensive discipline; strong domestic success George Graham League titles (1989, 1991); European Cup Winners’ Cup (1994)
1996–2018 (Arsène Wenger Era) 1996–2018 (Arsène Wenger Era) Arsene Wenger “Invincibles” 2003–04 season (unbeaten league); multiple FA Cups
2006 (European Highlight) Reached UEFA Champions League final Arsène Wenger, Thierry Henry First and only UCL final (lost to Barcelona)
2006–Present (Emirates Era) Moved to Emirates Stadium; financial transition period Arsène Wenger Continued FA Cup success; rebuilding phase
2019–Present (Modern Era) Youth development and squad rebuild under Arteta Mikel Arteta FA Cup 2020; renewed title challenges
