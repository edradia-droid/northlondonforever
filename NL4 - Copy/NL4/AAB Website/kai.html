Kai Havertz

Kai Havertz (born June 11, 1999, Aachen, Germany) is a German professional footballer who plays as a forward for Arsenal F.C. and the Germany national football team. Known for his height, technique, and versatility, he can operate as an attacking midfielder, false nine, or center-forward.

Early life and rise

Havertz began playing at Alemannia Mariadorf and later joined Alemannia Aachen before entering Bayer 04 Leverkusen’s academy at age 11. Making his Bundesliga debut at 17 in 2016, he became Leverkusen’s youngest-ever player and scorer. Across four seasons he tallied 46 goals and 31 assists in 150 appearances, earning Bundesliga Team of the Season honors (2018-19)

Chelsea years and major honors

In September 2020, Havertz moved to Chelsea for a reported €80 million. He wrote club history by scoring the winning goal in the 2021 UEFA Champions League Final against Manchester City F.C. and later netting in the 2022 FIFA Club World Cup Final versus Palmeiras . He contributed 32 goals in 139 matches across competitions for the Blues.

Arsenal role and performance

Joining Arsenal in June 2023, Havertz initially played in midfield before thriving as a striker under Mikel Arteta. In the 2023/24 season he registered 13 Premier League goals and 7 assists, earning two Player of the Month awards . His height, aerial ability, and off-ball movement have become key to Arsenal’s fluid attack

International career

Since debuting for Germany in 2018, Havertz has represented the national team at UEFA Euro 2020, the 2022 FIFA World Cup, and UEFA Euro 2024, scoring in each tournament. He remains a regular in Julian Nagelsmann’s squad

Off the pitch

Married to German model Sophia Weber (2024), Havertz is a Puma ambassador and one of the faces of its FUTURE boot line . He founded the Kai Havertz Stiftung charity, supporting animal welfare and social causes . Known for his calm personality, he is also an accomplished pianist and animal lover.
