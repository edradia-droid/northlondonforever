Mesut Özil

Mesut Özil is a retired German professional soccer player renowned for his creativity and precise passing as an attacking midfielder. Best known for his spell with Arsenal FC and for winning the 2014 FIFA World Cup with Germany, he was widely regarded as one of the finest playmakers of his generation.

Early career and rise

Özil emerged from the youth ranks at Schalke 04 and rose to prominence with Werder Bremen, showcasing an eye for the decisive final pass. His performances at the 2010 World Cup earned him a transfer to Real Madrid C.F., where he became a key creator under José Mourinho, providing assists for stars such as Cristiano Ronaldo .

Arsenal years

Joining Arsenal F.C. in 2013 for a club-record £42.5 million, Özil transformed the team’s attacking play with his vision and control. He helped Arsenal win four FA Cups (2014, 2015, 2017, 2020) and was praised by Arsène Wenger as a “complete athlete” and team leader .

International career and controversies

With Germany, Özil played a pivotal role in the 2014 World Cup triumph. He retired from international duty in 2018, citing racial discrimination from federation officials after criticism of his form and heritage .

Later life and ventures

After leaving Arsenal in 2021, he joined Fenerbahçe S.K. and later İstanbul Başakşehir F.K., before retiring in 2023. Post-career, Özil has focused on fitness and entrepreneurship, including his esports organization M10 Esports, and became a shareholder in Club Necaxa .
