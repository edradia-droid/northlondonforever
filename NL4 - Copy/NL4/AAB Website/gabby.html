Gabriel Magalhães

Gabriel dos Santos Magalhães (born December 19, 1997) is a Brazilian professional footballer who plays as a central defender for Arsenal F.C. and the Brazil national football team. Known for his aerial strength, physical presence, and composed distribution, he has become one of the Premier League’s most dependable defenders.

Early Career and Move to Europe

Gabriel began his youth career at Avaí FC in Brazil, making his senior debut in 2016 and helping the team earn promotion to the Brazilian Série A. In 2017 he joined LOSC Lille in France, where he developed through loan spells at ES Troyes AC and GNK Dinamo Zagreb. His 2019–20 season at Lille established him as a rising defensive talent in Ligue 1.

Arsenal Career

In September 2020, Gabriel signed for Arsenal for around £27 million. He scored on his Premier League debut and quickly cemented his place in Mikel Arteta’s starting lineup. His partnership with William Saliba became central to Arsenal’s resurgence, combining aggression and composure to anchor one of the league’s strongest defenses. Gabriel contributed goals from set pieces and helped Arsenal mount consecutive title challenges in 2023–24 and 2024–25.

International Career

Gabriel represented Brazil at under-20 and under-23 levels before earning his senior call-up in 2023. He debuted in World Cup qualifying and featured in the Copa América 2024. His performances secured him a place among Brazil’s defensive mainstays for future tournaments.

Style and Impact

Gabriel is recognized for his physicality, aerial dominance, and ability to play out from the back, fitting seamlessly into Arsenal’s possession-based system. Once prone to rash challenges, he has matured into a composed leader on the pitch. His presence has been pivotal in transforming Arsenal’s defense into a Premier League benchmark.
