THE CHARLIE STORY

Arsenal Heroics

Rising through Arsenal’s youth system, George made his first-team debut in 1969. He became an important figure in the club’s Inter-Cities Fairs Cup success before reaching legendary status in the 1970–71 season.

The 1971 FA Cup Final

In the 1971 FA Cup Final against Liverpool, George produced the decisive moment in extra time, striking a spectacular goal that helped Arsenal win 2–1 and complete the club’s first League and FA Cup Double. His arms-outstretched celebration at Wembley became an enduring Arsenal image.

Derby, Later Clubs & Legacy

George joined Derby County in 1975 and later played for Southampton and Nottingham Forest, as well as clubs in Australia, the United States and Hong Kong. He was capped once by England. His flair, long-range shooting and rebellious personality made him one of the defining Arsenal characters of the 1970s.
