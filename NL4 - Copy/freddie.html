THE FREDDIE STORY

Club Career & Arsenal Legacy

Ljungberg joined Arsenal in 1998 under manager Arsène Wenger and quickly established himself as one of the club's most dangerous attacking players. His pace, intelligent movement and ability to arrive late in the box made him a crucial part of Arsenal's midfield.

His standout campaign came in 2001–02, when he scored 17 goals as Arsenal won the Premier League and FA Cup Double. He was also an important member of the 2003–04 "Invincibles" side that completed an entire Premier League season unbeaten.

International Career

Ljungberg represented Sweden from 1998 to 2008, earning 75 international caps and scoring 14 goals. He became captain of his country and represented Sweden at UEFA Euro 2000, Euro 2004 and Euro 2008, as well as the 2002 and 2006 FIFA World Cups.

Later Career & Coaching

After leaving Arsenal in 2007, Ljungberg continued his career with West Ham United, Seattle Sounders, Chicago Fire, Celtic, Shimizu S-Pulse and Mumbai City before retiring from professional football in 2015.

He later returned to Arsenal as a coach, working with the club's youth teams before becoming part of the senior coaching staff. In 2019, he briefly served as Arsenal's interim head coach.
