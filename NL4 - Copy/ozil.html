THE Mesut STORY

Early Career & Rise

Özil emerged from the youth ranks at Schalke 04 and rose to prominence with Werder Bremen. His performances at the 2010 World Cup earned him a transfer to Real Madrid, where he became a key creator under José Mourinho.

Arsenal Years

Joining Arsenal in 2013 for a then-club-record fee, Özil transformed the team’s attacking play with his vision and control. He helped Arsenal win four FA Cups and became one of the club’s most memorable modern playmakers.

International Career & Later Life

Özil played a pivotal role in Germany’s 2014 World Cup triumph before retiring from international duty in 2018. After leaving Arsenal in 2021, he played in Turkey before retiring in 2023.
