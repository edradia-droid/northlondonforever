02 / HIS JOURNEY

THE ØDEGAARD STORY

Teenage Prodigy

Ødegaard emerged as one of Norway's greatest teenage talents. At just 15, he became the youngest player and goalscorer in Norway's top division before earning a senior international debut.

Real Madrid to Arsenal

He signed for Real Madrid in 2015 and gained valuable experience through loan spells with Heerenveen, Vitesse, and Real Sociedad. He joined Arsenal on loan in January 2021 before making the move permanent later that year.

Captain & Leader

Named Arsenal captain in 2022, Ødegaard became one of Mikel Arteta's most influential players. His precise passing, pressing, spatial awareness, and creativity have made him a key part of Arsenal's positional play.
