02 / HIS JOURNEY

THE LIAM BRADY STORY

Arsenal Beginnings

Brady joined Arsenal as a teenager and made his first-team debut in 1973. His exceptional technique, intelligence and creativity quickly established him as one of the most exciting young players at Highbury. He developed into the creative fulcrum of Arsenal's team throughout the second half of the 1970s.

The 1979 FA Cup Triumph

Brady played a central role in Arsenal's memorable 1979 FA Cup triumph. In the dramatic final against Manchester United, he produced the celebrated assist that helped Arsenal secure a thrilling 3–2 victory at Wembley.

PFA Player of the Year

In 1979, Brady was recognised as the PFA Players' Player of the Year. He became the first non-British player to receive the prestigious award, underlining the extraordinary influence he had established in English football.

Italian Adventure

Brady left Arsenal in 1980 for Juventus, where his composed passing and technical quality helped him win consecutive Serie A titles in 1980–81 and 1981–82. He later represented Sampdoria, Inter Milan and Ascoli before returning to England.

Ireland International

Brady represented the Republic of Ireland from 1974 to 1990, earning 72 international caps and scoring nine goals. His international career reflected the same elegance, creativity and technical quality that made him famous at Arsenal.

Coaching & Arsenal Academy

After his playing career, Brady moved into coaching and management, taking charge of Celtic and Brighton. He returned to Arsenal in 1996 as Head of Youth Development, helping oversee the development of future Arsenal stars including Ashley Cole and Jack Wilshere.

Broadcasting Career

Brady also became a respected football broadcaster, serving as an RTÉ Sport pundit for many years. His ability to analyse the game with intelligence and insight made him a familiar and respected voice in Irish football.

A Lasting Legacy

Liam Brady remains one of Arsenal's most naturally gifted footballers. His elegant left foot, vision, passing and creativity made him a symbol of technical excellence and helped establish his place among the club's most celebrated legends.
