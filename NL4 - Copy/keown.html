Martin Keown is a retired English footballer best known as a commanding central defender for Arsenal F.C. and the England national team. Renowned for his tenacity, intelligence, and leadership, he was a cornerstone of Arsenal’s defense during its most successful modern era, including the unbeaten “Invincibles” season of 2003–04.

THE MARTIN STORY

Arsenal Career & Achievements

Keown began his Arsenal career in the mid-1980s before spells at Aston Villa and Everton, returning to Arsenal in 1993. Under George Graham and Arsène Wenger, he matured into a key defensive figure alongside Tony Adams and later Sol Campbell.

Titles & The Invincibles

His disciplined, physical defending and organizational skills helped Arsenal to three Premier League titles and three FA Cups, culminating in his contribution to the 2003–04 “Invincibles” season when Arsenal went unbeaten in the league.

England, Style & Legacy

For England, Keown was capped 43 times between 1992 and 2002 and appeared at major tournaments including the 1998 and 2002 World Cups. His game was defined by anticipation, positioning, aggression and intelligence. After retirement he moved into coaching and punditry.
