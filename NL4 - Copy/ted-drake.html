THE TED DRAKE STORY

Arsenal Career & Achievements

Joining Arsenal from Southampton in 1934, Drake became the spearhead of George Allison’s championship side. His 1934–35 season tally of 44 goals remains among the club’s highest single-season totals. He also scored the winning goal in the 1936 FA Cup Final against Sheffield United.

A Remarkable Goalscorer

Drake’s seven-goal performance against Aston Villa in 1935 remains a joint record for the most goals by one player in a top-flight English league match. His strength, courage and finishing made him one of Arsenal’s defining forwards of the pre-war era.

England, Management & Legacy

Between 1934 and 1938 Drake earned five caps for England and scored six goals. After wartime service in the Royal Air Force, he moved into management and led Chelsea to their first league title in 1954–55, becoming the first person to win England’s top league as both player and manager.
