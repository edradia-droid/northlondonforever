THE GEORGE MALE STORY

Club Career

Male joined Arsenal as an amateur in 1929 and turned professional in 1930. Initially a left-sided player, he moved to right back in 1932 and became a defensive mainstay, contributing to four League titles and the 1935–36 FA Cup.

International & Wartime Service

Male made his England debut against Scotland in 1934 and earned 19 caps, six as captain. During the Second World War he played wartime fixtures for Arsenal while serving in the Royal Air Force in Palestine.

Later Life & Legacy

After retiring, Male coached Arsenal’s youth and reserve sides and later worked as a scout. He remained connected with the club for decades and is remembered as one of English football’s pioneering full backs and a model of club loyalty.
