THE IAN STORY

Playing Career

Wright rose from humble beginnings in south London, turning professional late at age 21 with Crystal Palace. His breakthrough came at Arsenal after signing in 1991. He helped Arsenal to a league-and-cup double in 1998 and became the club’s record scorer before Thierry Henry surpassed him in 2005.

International & Cultural Impact

Although he earned 33 caps for England, Wright never played in a major tournament. He became a cultural icon of the Premier League’s early era, inspiring a generation of Black British footballers and fans.

Media, Advocacy & Legacy

Post-retirement, Wright became a fixture on BBC’s Match of the Day and ITV’s football coverage. He has also used his platform to speak on racism, domestic violence, men’s mental health, and women’s football.
