02 / HIS JOURNEY

THE LEHMANN STORY

Club Career & Arsenal Legacy

Lehmann began his professional career with Schalke 04, where he established himself as an outstanding goalkeeper and helped the club win the 1997 UEFA Cup. He later played for AC Milan and Borussia Dortmund, winning the Bundesliga with Dortmund in 2002.

In 2003, Lehmann joined Arsenal and immediately became the club's first-choice goalkeeper. His performances were crucial during the 2003–04 season as Arsenal completed an entire Premier League campaign unbeaten.

International Career

Lehmann represented Germany at international level and became the country's first-choice goalkeeper for the 2006 FIFA World Cup. His famous penalty saves against Argentina helped Germany reach the semi-finals.

He also represented Germany at UEFA Euro 2008, where the team reached the final. His rivalry with Oliver Kahn became one of the defining goalkeeper battles in German football.

Later Career & Coaching

After leaving Arsenal in 2008, Lehmann returned to Germany with VfB Stuttgart. He later came out of retirement for a short return to Arsenal in 2011, making a memorable final appearance before ending his playing career.

Following retirement, Lehmann moved into coaching, punditry and football analysis. He also worked with Arsenal's coaching staff and gained experience in German football.
