THE MARTINELLI STORY

From Futsal to Arsenal

Martinelli began playing futsal before developing at Ituano in Brazil. He made his professional debut at 16 before Arsenal signed him in July 2019. His first season included 10 goals and an FA Cup winner's medal.

Development Under Arteta

After recovering from a knee injury, Martinelli established himself as an important part of Mikel Arteta's system. From the left wing, his acceleration, pressing, direct running, and finishing became major weapons for Arsenal.

Brazil & International Career

Martinelli chose to represent Brazil internationally. He won Olympic gold with Brazil's under-23 team at Tokyo 2020, later making his senior debut and featuring at the 2022 FIFA World Cup and 2024 Copa América.
