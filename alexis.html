THE Alexis STORY

Early Career & Rise

Sánchez began his professional career at Cobreloa before moving to Udinese in Italy. Loan spells at Colo-Colo and River Plate helped develop his attacking flair, and his performances in Serie A attracted Europe’s elite clubs.

Arsenal Years

Joining Arsenal in 2014, Sánchez became a Premier League star under Arsène Wenger. He scored 60 league goals in three-and-a-half seasons, won two FA Cups and two Community Shields, and became a major fan favorite.

Later Career & International Success

A 2018 move to Manchester United was followed by a resurgence at Inter Milan, where he helped win the 2020–21 Serie A title. Internationally, he was instrumental in Chile’s consecutive Copa América triumphs in 2015 and 2016.
