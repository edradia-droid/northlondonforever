HOW NL4 CALCULATES THIS

V12.9 blends current performance with calibrated preseason club-strength priors, Elo-style ratings, recent form and home/away evidence. Newly promoted clubs start from a lower strength prior than established title contenders, but that prior fades continuously and reaches zero after roughly 20 league matches per club, so exceptional real performance can fully take over. The rest of the season is simulated 25,000 times using every real remaining Premier League fixture currently stored in Supabase. Home advantage and exact opponent strength are applied.
