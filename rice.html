02 / HIS JOURNEY

THE DECLAN RICE STORY

West Ham Rise

Rice joined Chelsea's academy at seven before being released at 14. He later developed at West Ham United, making his Premier League debut in 2017 and becoming one of the club's most important players.

Arsenal Record Signing

Rice joined Arsenal in July 2023 for £105 million, becoming the club's record signing. Under Mikel Arteta, he developed into a versatile box-to-box midfielder, combining defensive recovery with attacking influence.

England & Recognition

After representing the Republic of Ireland before switching allegiance, Rice became a regular for England. His progressive passing, interceptions, physicality, and ball-carrying have established him as one of Europe's leading midfielders.
