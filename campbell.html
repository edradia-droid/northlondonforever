THE SOL STORY

Club Career & Arsenal Legacy

Campbell began his professional career with Tottenham Hotspur before making the controversial move across North London to Arsenal in 2001. His arrival brought experience, physical strength and leadership to Arsène Wenger's defence.

At Arsenal, Campbell won two Premier League titles and three FA Cups. He became a central figure in the 2003–04 Invincibles side, helping Arsenal complete an entire Premier League season without defeat.

International Career

Campbell represented England between 1996 and 2007, earning 73 caps and scoring once. He appeared at six consecutive major international tournaments, from UEFA Euro 1996 through the 2006 FIFA World Cup.

His consistency and commanding performances made him one of England's most respected defenders of his era.

Later Career & Coaching

After leaving Arsenal, Campbell captained Portsmouth to FA Cup success in 2008 before later playing for Notts County and Newcastle United. He eventually retired from professional football.

Campbell later moved into management, taking charge of Macclesfield Town and Southend United while also becoming an advocate for greater diversity and opportunity within football.
