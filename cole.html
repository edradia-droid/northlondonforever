THE ASHLEY STORY

Arsenal Career & Invincibles

A product of Arsenal's academy, Cole made his first-team debut in 1999 and quickly established himself as the club's first-choice left-back under Arsène Wenger.

He played a major role in Arsenal's 2001–02 Double triumph and was an important member of the legendary 2003–04 Invincibles team that went unbeaten throughout the Premier League season.

International Career

Cole became one of England's most reliable defenders, earning 107 international caps between 2001 and 2014. He represented England at three FIFA World Cups and several European Championships.

His defensive ability and consistency earned him widespread recognition as one of the finest left-backs of his generation.

Later Career & Coaching

Cole joined Chelsea in 2006 and continued to collect major honours, including Premier League, FA Cup, UEFA Champions League and Europa League titles.

Following retirement, he moved into coaching and worked with England's youth teams. He was part of the setup that helped England's Under-21 side win the 2023 European Championship.
